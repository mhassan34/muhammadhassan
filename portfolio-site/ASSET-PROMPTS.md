# Generated project artwork

Existing raster assets are stored in the public/assets directory. The project images were edited from their previous raster versions to simplify the composition while retaining each scene and making the result feel closer to a restrained Unreal Engine gameplay capture.

## `project-umg.png`

Create a 16:9 high-end Unreal Engine UMG settings-interface showcase. Preserve a believable settings layout and production-ready controls inside crisp charcoal panels with acid-lime selected states. Place the interface over a cinematic dusk amusement-park environment. Keep it professional, highly legible, square-edged, and free of logos or a large title.

## `project-splat.png`

Create a cinematic 16:9 industrial restaurant interior transitioning from a photoreal Unreal Engine render on the left into an accurate golden Gaussian Splat point cloud on the right. Use a subtle acid-lime scan boundary, warm neutral lighting, visible ducts and seating, strong technical credibility, and no logos or text.

## `project-multiplayer.png`

Create a polished 16:9 Unreal Engine multiplayer shared-world scene at a virtual festival. Show a third-person female player in the lower-left foreground, a convincing crowd of networked participants, a large performance stage, and warm late-afternoon light. Keep the image cinematic and realistic, without UI, logos, or text.
